    How to bill

    1. Customer – type or 🎤 the name; the mobile fills itself for known customers, or say it: “Ramesh Kumar 98765 43210”.

    2. Items – tap a quick item button, or 🎤 one item per sentence: “Haldi 100 gram 5 kg”. After tapping a button, just say the quantity: “5 kg”. The last number is the quantity; an earlier number with a unit is the pack size. “hatao” deletes the last item.

    3. Items Complete – Enter Rates – the app shows one item at a time. Say or type the rate, e.g. “200”, then Next. “same” = last rate, “skip”, “back”.

    4. Review & Save – fix anything marked red/⚠ right there, check the total, then Confirm & Save Memo.

    5. PDF – share it on WhatsApp, download or print. Reprint any time from the Day Book.

    Corrections by voice – “change quantity to 5”, “change unit to carton”, “change rate to 200”, “repeat last bill”.
